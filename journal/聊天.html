Rain taps the roof, a soft, uneven drum,
then settles in to stay.
The gutters hum, the sidewalks turn to glass,
and every window holds a little gray.

Somewhere a kettle whistles, someone stays
a while longer than they planned.
Rain doesn't rush. It teaches
how to let a day fall through your hands.
